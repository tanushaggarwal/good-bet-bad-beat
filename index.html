// 03 — Approach

How I think about poker decisions.

Winning a hand is easy. Judging your own decision honestly is the hard part.

That is the habit this site teaches.

01

Decision first, result second

Cover the result and ask what I knew when I acted. Call the decision good or bad. Only then do I look at how the hand ended.

02

Honesty runs both ways

A bad decision that wins goes in a bad-decision box, same as one that loses. Luck cannot excuse a mistake, and a win cannot hide one.

03

Small enough to keep

One or two hands, five minutes, right after the session. A small habit I keep beats a perfect one I skip.

04

Name the source

Every idea here came from someone, and I link the page I read. I am still learning, so I say so.
